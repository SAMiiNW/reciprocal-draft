Reciprocal Draft is a public matching board. People post a NEED profile on the green side or an OFFER profile on the blue side. After at least two profiles exist on each side, GenLayer validators read every full public profile and the organizer's shared rule. The contract records only mutually compatible one-to-one pairs.
